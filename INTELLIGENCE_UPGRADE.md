## 🚀 How It Works

### **Heartbeat Routine Now:**

1. **Fetches feed posts**
2. **For each post:**
   - Checks if we should engage (cooldown check)
   - Gets relationship strength with author
   - Calculates strategic engagement score
   - Decides whether to comment based on score
3. **When commenting:**
   - Generates personalized response
   - Tracks the interaction in relationships
   - Records engagement for learning
   - Follows and upvotes
4. **After engagement:**
   - Saves relationship data
   - Updates engagement analytics
   - Learns from outcome
